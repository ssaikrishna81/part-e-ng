About the ServiceUniverse Portal

ServiceUniverse brings together essential public services—Opal top ups, vaccination bookings, and water billing—into a single, trusted experience designed for busy residents.

Our mission

We want to remove the friction from everyday civic tasks. Instead of juggling multiple accounts and websites, ServiceUniverse gives you one dashboard to complete important errands in minutes.

  • Secure authentication shared across every participating agency.
  • Consistent navigation and accessibility on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
  • Live service availability indicators so you always know what to expect.

What you can do today

Travel with confidence

Recharge your Opal card, view recent journeys, and set up automatic top-ups without leaving the portal.

Stay on top of vaccinations

Book appointments, download certificates, and check eligibility for upcoming public health programs.

Manage your household

Review water usage, pay balances, and lodge burst pipe alerts with your local utilities in a few clicks.

Built for growth

ServiceUniverse is engineered with a modular gateway so new departments can plug in quickly. Our roadmap includes digital licensing, community safety reporting, and high-demand seasonal services to keep the city moving.
